---
name: grilling
description: Interview the user relentlessly about a plan or design. Use when the user wants to stress-test a plan before building, or uses any 'grill' trigger phrases.
---

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan until we reach a shared understanding.

Walk down each branch of the design tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one. For each question, provide your recommended answer.

For questions where the options or follow-ups depend on a prior answer, ask them one at a time and wait for the answer before continuing.

For questions that are fully independent of each other and of any unanswered questions, batch up to 4 of them together using the AskUserQuestion tool. The structured format (each question gets its own header and options) keeps it readable without being bewildering.

If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase, explore the codebase instead.
